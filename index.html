Medixus: Scaling Africa's Medical Social Network

The Challenge

Healthcare professionals across Africa faced critical barriers to knowledge sharing and collaboration. Clinicians working in resource-constrained environments struggled to access timely second opinions for complex cases, stay current with medical developments, and connect with specialists outside their immediate network.

This isolation resulted in diagnostic uncertainty, delayed patient care decisions, and missed opportunities for collaborative learning—ultimately impacting patient outcomes across the continent.

Noveltek: Streamlining Clinical Trial Recruitment in East Africa

The Problem: A Broken Recruitment Pipeline

Clinical trials in East Africa faced recruitment challenges that delayed life-changing research and limited access to treatments. Community Health Promoters lacked standardized tools to identify and track potential participants efficiently. Manual paper-based processes created bottlenecks, with participant data lost between community outreach and research site enrollment.
